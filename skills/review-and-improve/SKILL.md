# Review and improve

Read `../SESSION.md`, `../stop-slop/SKILL.md` and `../../docs/WORKFLOW-CONTRACTS.md` (Materials, Sources and facts, Permissions). Reuse startup/sync coverage from the parent run.

**Inputs:** selected canonical snapshot/date range, coverage/import notes, application/material evidence, facts/source registry and user priorities.

1. Resolve the review scope: outcome analysis, work/experience review or both. If current outcomes are needed, run a bounded authorized sync or reuse its checked phase; a named historical snapshot remains historical. Completion: date range, checked sources, missing coverage, revision and separate discovered/confirmed counts are explicit.
2. Audit the evidence before comparison using the Materials contract: preparation fields, application evidence, managed files and original receipts/forms. Completion: saved bytes, submitted-file proof, template ancestry, job links and answers have separate coverage counts; source-present data is not called missing because a structured field is empty.
3. Compare relevant role families and exact resume files with denominators, association bases, pending time and unknown dates. Keep approved template ancestry as a separate cohort. Review repeated employers, offers and pay terms without double-counting geographic listings or treating a range as an offer. Completion: totals reconcile to the canonical snapshot and small/nonrandom samples carry no causal wording claim.
4. Trace interview/decision feedback to the exact source and exercise. Preserve user clarifications, unknown stages and later correspondence; a request to reapply is not an employer reopening. Completion: employer feedback, user report and inference are distinguishable, without generalizing one demo into a diagnosis of the user's product or ability.
5. For new work, use `../uncover-evidence/SKILL.md` incremental mode to inspect relevant artifacts and ask about missing personal decisions/results. Completion: candidate experience and exact proposed external wording have distinct evidence/approval status.
6. Choose one useful next improvement based on the supported finding: record recovery, targeting, wording review or focused practice on the actual exercise. Give a concrete check and review date, without creating a reminder unless requested. Show the relevant app record or private evidence review when useful. Completion: one actionable next step and resumable outputs are saved.

**Outputs:** private dated analysis with record/source IDs, denominators/limits, evidence questions and one improvement with check/follow-up date.

**Approval gates:** Shared Permissions apply. Analysis and candidate facts do not create new claims or submission authorization. Approve materially changed external wording or scope before use.

**Completion checks:** coverage and counts reconcile; exact file evidence and template ancestry remain separate; feedback stays scoped; uncertainty and pending time are visible; the improvement has a source, check and next review date.
